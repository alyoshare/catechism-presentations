The word that answers it

What does "inspired" actually mean?

All Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness.2 Timothy 3:16
Men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit.2 Peter 1:21

θεόπνευστος

theos (God) + pneuo (to breathe)
= "God-breathed"

What the Church teaches

Same word, spelled out

God is the author of ScriptureCCC 105
He chose human authors to write what He wantedCCC 106
Inspiration = God-breathedThe Holy Spirit carried them as they wrote
The Bible teaches truth for our salvationCCC 107

God is the author. The authors are real. Both things are true at once.

Why trust it?

Let's compare the evidence

Ancient work Written Earliest copies Copies known
Plato's works ~400 BC 1,200 years later 7
Virgil's Aeneid ~20 BC 350 years later A few
New Testament ~50–100 AD 30–50 years later 5,000+ (Greek)

If you trust your history textbook about Plato,
you should absolutely trust the Gospels.

The "so what?"

But Jesus claimed to be God…

If Jesus really claimed divinity, there are only three options. Pick your character:

Option A
Liar
He knew it was false and said it anyway.
Option B
Lunatic
He genuinely believed a delusion.
Option C
Lord
He really is God.

His teachings and His impact rule out the other two.

Nobody calls a liar or a madman the most influential teacher in human history —
and then writes the parables that keep happening anyway.

The right way to read Scripture

1 Read it prayerfully — with the Holy Spirit
2 Read the whole message, not isolated verses
3 Interpret it with Jesus Christ at the center
4 Trust the Magisterium (Pope & Bishops) for authentic interpretation
"Interpretation of the inspired Scripture must be attentive above all to what God wants to reveal through the sacred authors for our salvation." CCC 137
